Privacy Notice

What happens to your phone number when you register for Shaveers launch updates.

Version 2026-11-12 · Published 12 November 2026

1. Information we collect

When you register, we collect your phone number, the time you signed up, the method of contact you agreed to (SMS and WhatsApp), the waitlist source, and the version of the terms and privacy notice you accepted. We do not ask for your name, payment card, address, or email on this waitlist.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to manage launch notifications, avoid duplicate registrations, and keep a record of your consent. The number will be used for Shaveers launch-related messages through SMS or WhatsApp, as stated next to the signup box.

3. Where it is stored and who can access it

We process registrations on the server and store them in private Vercel storage. Authorized Shaveers administrators can access registrations. Hosting, database and messaging service providers may process information as needed to operate the service. We do not put the registration list on the public website or sell phone numbers to advertisers.

4. Retention

We keep waitlist information only while it is useful for the launch notifications, and review the list after the launch campaign. You can request removal sooner.

5. Your choices

You can ask Shaveers to remove your phone number from the launch list, or ask what waitlist information we hold, by messaging us on WhatsApp. These requests are handled manually and we may ask for reasonable verification before disclosing or removing another person's information.

6. Security and verification

Your browser sends submissions over HTTPS. However, a phone number typed into a form is not proof of ownership. We do not currently verify registrations by one-time passcode, and no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Updates and contact

We may revise this notice if the data handling changes. Contact Shaveers via WhatsApp with privacy questions.
