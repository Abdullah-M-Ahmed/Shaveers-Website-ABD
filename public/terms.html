Waitlist Terms

Please read these terms before giving us your number for Shaveers launch notifications.

Version 2026-11-12 · Published 12 November 2026

1. What you are signing up for

By joining this waitlist, you ask Shaveers to notify you about the launch of our clothing collection and related availability announcements. Joining is free, does not reserve a product, and does not place an order or guarantee a specific launch date.

2. Your phone number and permission

You confirm that the phone number you provide is yours, or that you are authorized to receive messages at it. When you tick the consent box and submit the form, you give Shaveers permission to store the number and send you launch-related SMS and/or WhatsApp messages. Message frequency depends on the launch campaign; we will not use this signup for unrelated third-party promotions.

The checkbox is optional until you choose to register, never pre-selected, and required to join the waitlist. Please read the Privacy Notice for information on storage and use.

3. Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw consent or request removal at any time by contacting Shaveers on WhatsApp and asking to be removed from the launch waitlist. Removal requests are processed manually. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before your request.

4. Changes to the waitlist

Shaveers may update this service or these terms. If we materially expand how we use your number, we will seek fresh consent where appropriate rather than treating your original launch-waitlist signup as blanket permission.

5. Questions

For questions about this signup, contact Shaveers through our official WhatsApp contact.
